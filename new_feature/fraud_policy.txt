FRAUDLENS PAYMENT VERIFICATION AND FRAUD POLICY

POLICY TYPE
This is a demonstration policy for a local fraud-detection application.
It is not an official bank policy or a regulatory standard.

1. VERIFIED USERS

The configured verified users are:

- RAGHUL
- VISWA
- YAMUNA

Verification in this demonstration is based only on membership in the
application's configured list. It does not verify real-world identity.

A verified sender may proceed under the configured demo rules when the
receiver is also verified and the transaction does not meet a condition
that requires review.

2. UNKNOWN SENDERS

A sender who is not present in the configured verified-user list is
not authorized to proceed under the demo rules.

The application should display:

- Sender not verified.
- Suspicious or unverified account activity.
- The configured rule-based risk score.
- The specific reasons for the decision.

An unknown account is not automatically proven to be fraudulent.

3. UNKNOWN RECEIVERS

A payment to an unverified receiver should be held for review under
this demonstration policy, even when the sender is verified.

The application should identify the receiver verification issue and
explain why additional verification is needed.

4. DEMONSTRATION AMOUNT THRESHOLDS

INR 10,000 to below INR 25,000:
Add a review risk signal.

INR 25,000 or more:
Add a high-amount risk signal and require review.

These amounts are configurable examples and are not official banking
limits. Amount alone does not establish fraud.

5. RISK SCORE

The application's displayed risk score is calculated using explicit
weighted rules for sender verification, receiver verification,
transaction amount, and optional experimental model signals.

The displayed value is capped at 100.

This is a rule-based demonstration score. It is not a calibrated
probability of fraud and should not be described as one.

6. PAYMENT DECISIONS

APPROVED BY DEMO RULES:
The sender and receiver are configured as verified, and no high-amount
review condition has been triggered.

SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY — REVIEW RECEIVER:
The sender is verified but the receiver is not configured as verified.

HIGH-VALUE PAYMENT — REVIEW REQUIRED:
Both accounts are verified, but the transaction reaches the configured
high-amount threshold.

BLOCKED — SENDER NOT VERIFIED:
The sender is not configured as verified.

These are simulated application decisions. The application does not
connect to a real payment network and does not move, block, or reverse money.

7. MODEL LIMITATIONS

The standard Kaggle credit card dataset contains anonymized numerical
features, including V1-V28, Amount, Time, and Class.

Class 0 means historically labelled legitimate.
Class 1 means historically labelled fraud.

The dataset does not supply the real account identities used in the
FraudLens payment form.

When required features are missing, filling them with training medians
does not provide a reliable prediction for a real transaction.

The model score must be described as experimental when these features
are not supplied.

8. RAG REQUIREMENTS

Retrieve relevant policy passages for the transaction and question.

Use the retrieved passages as policy evidence and the transaction
fields as case evidence.

The AI should explain the actual decision, triggered signals, risk
score calculation, and next appropriate action.

Cite only passages actually retrieved from this policy.

If the policy does not support a claim, say so.

Do not invent account history, identity verification, transaction
history, device information, or completed checks.

9. PRIVACY AND HUMAN REVIEW

Never request a full card number, CVV, PIN, password, or OTP.

Use authorized records to verify identities and investigate suspicious
transactions.

Escalate uncertain cases to an authorized reviewer.

10. DISCLAIMER

FraudLens is a demonstration system. Production payment approval requires
secure identity verification, approved risk controls, access control,
audit logging, model validation, and integration with an authorized
payment system.
